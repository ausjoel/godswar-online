Dodge and Gaia Care

Gaia Care V adds 3,000 dodge and 1,000 critical resistance for 10 seconds, with a 90-second cooldown. It is short burst protection. Additional dodge stops reducing hit chance after reaching the 5% PvP floor against a particular attacker.

Flat Lifedrain and percentage Vampiric

Roly Poly Lifedrain VI can supply flat 670 healing per successful hit at pet rank 90. The separate project-authored Vampiric VI supplies 20% of committed damage. At equal hit counts, 20% overtakes flat 670 above 3,350 damage per hit, before rounding and caps.
