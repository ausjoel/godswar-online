The story

Medusa was once famous for her beauty, and vain enough to set herself against Athena. The goddess's curse turned her into a Gorgon whose gaze turns people to stone. Now she rules a jungle island with her sisters Stheno and Euryale and an army of Gorgon warriors, shamans and astrologers. Athena has sent Perseus to end her, but he needs heroes at his side.

Normal or Advanced

The Instance Caller offers two versions. Both use the same daily entries (3 a day in total, not 3 each).

  • Normal: the standard island.
  • Advanced: the same fight with monsters that have twice the HP and hit harder. It pays more Honour and is the only way to earn the speed titles.

Monster strength does not change with party size, so a full party of five clears much faster than a small one.

Reach 3,000 points to complete the island. Your score shows in the party panel. If you die you come back at the start point; if you leave early you get nothing.

Rewards

  • Honour for every run, rising with your score. A completed run pays more the faster it was, and Advanced pays more than Normal. Spend Honour at the Point Exchanger in the capitals.
  • Titles for completing Advanced quickly. Each title also adds to physical and magic attack and defence while you own it (the best one you own counts):
