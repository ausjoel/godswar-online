The story

Christina, a sorceress who later threw in her lot with Medusa, raised a small army of bandits, hired blades and Persian raiders, then bought the help of a skeleton warlord. Together they seized a busy harbour to choke the sea lanes the Greek cities depend on. Athens and Sparta have put aside their quarrel long enough to pay anyone who can drive them out.

Getting in

  • Bring a party of at least three, everyone level 50 to 90.
  • The leader asks the Instance Caller in either capital for Bay Under Attack. Members click their entry button.
  • The leader's level picks the harbour: below level 70 you get the lower-level version, from 70 the tougher one.

Inside

  • Work through the raiders' camp. Normal monsters are worth 1 point, elites 10 and bosses 50. Your score shows in the instance panel.
  • Two bosses wait for you: the Bloody Skeleton and, at the end, Christina herself.
  • Killing Christina ends the run, even if monsters are left. Clear the harbour first if you want the full reward.
  • The leader can end the run early by a party vote. Rewards are still paid for what you cleared.

Rewards

  • Clearance EXP. When the run ends, everyone inside gets EXP for the share of the harbour you cleared. A full clear is worth one whole level at your current level; half a clear is half a level, and so on. Killing only Christina is a small fraction.
  • Gear drops. Monsters drop level 50, 60 and 70 equipment with 3 or 4 random attributes.
  • If a server restart cuts your run short, your daily entry is given back.

On this server: the points, the clearance EXP and the gear drops are our own design. The old official game never published its numbers.
