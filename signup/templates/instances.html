Trials of Hercules (training ground)

Game model render

A cave full of giant spiders and scorpions where heroes train the way Hercules did. It is open to level 60 and up, solo or with a party of up to five, once a day, for 30 minutes.

  • Every monster appears once per run and does not come back: normals are worth 1 point, the 8 to 10 elites 10 points each.
  • The monsters are around level 75-90 and much tougher than their cousins outside: normals have five times the usual HP, elites ten times.
  • One full clear (every monster in the cave, once) completes the trial. You are paid EXP and talent points for how far you got; a full clear within 22½ minutes pays the most.
  • Revive near the entrance if you fall. Leaving early forfeits the reward; the leader can end the run by a vote and still be paid for the progress made.

This trial is our own take on the old Training Ground: the monsters, scoring and timings are designed for this server.

How entry works

  1. Form a party. The leader walks to the Instance Caller in Athens or Sparta (use Search under the mini-map) and picks the instance.
  2. Each member gets an entry button. Click it within the countdown to be taken in. A member who is a little late can still join while the run is open.
  3. Entering uses one of the day's entries for each member, whether the run is won or not.
  4. If you die inside, you come back at the start point and can keep going.
  5. Leaving before the end forfeits the rewards, so stay until the run finishes or the timer runs out.

Server restarts: if a restart ends a Bay Under Attack run, the day's entry is given back. For the other instances it is not.

Rewards in short

  • Trials of Hercules: EXP and talent points by progress.
  • Bay Under Attack: EXP for how much of the harbour you cleared, and gear with 3 or 4 random attributes from the monsters.
  • Medusa's Island: Honour from score and speed, spent at the Point Exchanger, and titles for the fastest clears.
  • Atlantis: HardPoints for the team score, also spent at the Point Exchanger, and a completion title.
  • Wonderland: Medals for every island, boss loot, island chests and titles.
