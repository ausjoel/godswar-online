Warrior

The shield-bearer. Warriors fight up close with a sword and shield and take the hits meant for everyone else. In a party the Warrior goes in first, keeps the monsters' attention and holds the line while the others deal the damage.

Plays like: sturdy, forgiving, steady damage. A good first class.
Skills you'll know: Light Chop, Sword Storm, Blood Claw.

Champion

The Warrior's reckless cousin. Champions trade the shield for a long spear and put everything into attack. They hit hard and fast, and they rely on killing things before things kill them.

Plays like: high damage up close, less room for mistakes.
Skills you'll know: Jab, Rage Spear, Ruined Strike.

Mage

Arcane fire at range. Mages wear light robes and burn their enemies from a distance, either one target at a time or a whole pack at once. They are fragile, so stay behind the front line.

Plays like: big area damage, careful positioning.
Skills you'll know: Lightball, Star Shower.

Priest

The gods' envoy. Priests heal the party, lift curses and harmful effects, and fight with the cold power of water and ice. A party with a Priest survives fights that would wipe a party without one.

Plays like: support first, with real damage of its own.
Skills you'll know: Ice Shot, Dripstone, Stalagmite.

Field skills

Every class has one ground-target skill that lays a burning field: cast it on a spot and it hits everything standing there ten times, once a second. The Warrior, Champion and Priest fields also hit enemy players, at half strength; Flame Blast hits monsters only. Learn ranks 1-5 at levels 10, 34, 58, 82 and 106.

Changing class

The Class Shifter in Athens and Sparta changes your class:

  • It is free up to and including level 100. Above that it takes one Class Shift Permit.
  • Take off everything you are wearing first: the Shifter refuses while you have gear equipped.
  • Your old class's skills are sealed and the new class's skills are unsealed. Talent levels from the old class come back as talent points to spend again.

Talents and skill books

Skills grow by learning higher ranks from skill books, which drop from monsters and instances. Talents are a separate tree that you fill with talent points earned as you play.

Levels 6 to 10. Every skill can go past level 5, up to level 10. The books for levels 6-10 are learnt at character levels 150, 160, 170, 185 and 200, and come from skill-book chests: each of the 19 world bosses has a 5% chance per kill to drop one for your class.
