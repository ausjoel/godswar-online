The story

Zeus condemned the Titan Atlas to hold up the sky for ever. Atlas broke free, turned against Olympus and went looking for a weapon. He found it in a peaceful land called Wonderland: he freed an army of trapped demon spirits, enslaved the people and built a citadel from which to strike at Greece. Every warrior of Athens and Sparta is needed to stop him.

How it plays

  • You start on the first island. Each island opens only when the one before it is cleared, which means beating the island's bosses.
  • When an island clears, a treasure chest appears and every member is paid Medals. Bosses can also drop rare items.
  • If you die, you return to the island's revival point and can try again.
  • Monster strength is the same whatever the party size and is set for a full party. Bring five if you can, with a Priest.
  • Entry is open every day until midnight, Brisbane time. One entry a day, used whether you win or not.

Rewards

  • Medals for every island cleared, 1,400 for a full run. Spend them at the Point Exchanger in the capitals.
  • Island chests and boss loot, including gems.
  • Titles for clearing the later islands.

Islands 6 to 8 opened properly only in November and very few parties have reached them yet. If something looks wrong up there, tell a GM what happened and roughly when.
