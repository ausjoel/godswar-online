Before you log in

  1. Make an account on this site. The username is lowercase letters and numbers only. Type it exactly like that in the game.
  2. Download the launcher for Mac or Windows and open it. It installs the game the first time and updates it every time after that.
  3. Log in with the same username and password. Pick the server, then an empty character slot.

One account can hold several characters. Your skill bars and window layout can be kept on the server too: on a Mac, hold Option while the game opens and save your account in the launcher.

Making your hero

You choose three things. Each can be changed later at an NPC in the capital:

  • Faction: Athens or Sparta. It decides your home city and who your enemies are. Changing it later costs Gold.
  • Class: Warrior, Champion, Mage or Priest. The Class Shifter changes it, free up to level 100.
  • Look: gender, face and hair.

Your first levels

  • Talk to the characters with a mark over their heads. The Newbie Guide in your capital starts the main quest chain, and quests are the quickest way to level early on.
  • Press the Search button under the mini-map to find any NPC by name, then click the result to walk there.
  • Put skills on the quick bar (1-7 and F1-F7) and use them.
  • Open the Rookie Guide button beside the mini-map: it pays growth rewards as you level. Gift rewards for time online come from the gift button.
  • Team up. Party members near each other share the experience and quest credit for kills.

Good habits

  • Check the event calendar: battlefields and spy raids run at set times (Brisbane time).
  • Sell what you don't need to merchants, and keep a few HP and MP potions on the quick bar.
  • Instances give one or more entries a day. A leader talks to the Instance Caller and the party is taken in together.
  • Stuck in a wall or lost? Ask in chat for a GM.
