Choosing a side

Athens and Sparta play the same: same classes, same quests, same instances. What changes is who stands beside you. Pick the side your friends are on: the other side is the enemy in player-versus-player fights.

Your capital has everything you need: merchants, the warehouse, the Transporter, the Instance Caller, the Class Shifter and the forge NPCs.

Where the two sides meet

  • Battlefields: Pindus, Ni Mini Valley and Lelantine Farm open at set times for faction-against-faction fights, with Reputation and titles for the winners. See the event calendar.
  • The Duel Arena is open all the time for fights between players.
  • Wonderland: on the fifth island your party faces the enemy faction's marshal. The marshal of your own city fights on your side.
  • Spy invasions hit the capitals on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday evenings. Defend your city wave by wave.

Changing faction

Changed your mind? A faction changer in the capital can move your character to the other side for a fee in Gold. Take off your gear first.
