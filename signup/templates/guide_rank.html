What a rank effect is

The armour and weapon you wear earn rank points from their quality and how far they are forged. Enough points light up a rank: higher ranks add stronger glows and, at the top, wings. Armour and weapon have separate ranks. Open your bag and hover over Armour Rank or Weapon Rank to see your points against the next rank.

The effect is only for looks. Choosing to show a lower rank never changes your stats or the rank you have earned.

Choose it from the bag (client 1.5.3)

  1. Open your bag.
  2. Click Armour Rank or Weapon Rank.
  3. Pick None, any rank from 1 up to the one your gear has earned, or Max (always show the full earned rank).

Your choice is saved on the server for that character, and the server sends it to players nearby too.

Or type /rank in chat

TypeWhat it does
/rankShows your earned and shown rank for armour and weapon.
/rank armour 4Show at most armour rank 4 (armor works too).
/rank weapon 2Show at most weapon rank 2.
/rank 3Set both armour and weapon to at most rank 3.
/rank weapon offHide the weapon effect (0 or none do the same).
/rank maxShow everything you have earned again.

The command line never appears in chat for other people. If you pick a rank higher than you have earned, you simply show your earned rank until your gear catches up.
