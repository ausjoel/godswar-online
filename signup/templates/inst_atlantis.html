The story

Atlantis sank beneath the sea long ago, and its treasure went down with it. Now a way in has been found, but the creatures living in the drowned halls attack anyone who comes near. Survive their waves and you may reach the city's secrets.

How it plays

  • Monsters come at you in waves. The more you kill, the bigger the reward.
  • Normal monsters are worth 1 point, elites 10 and bosses 50. 850 points completes the run.
  • You can go in alone. Clearing it solo earns a title of its own.
  • If you die you return to the start point and carry on. Leaving before the end means no reward.
  • You get 3 free entries a day. A fourth costs an Opal.

Mermaids

Look out for the Mermaids. They have a lot of HP, so bring a capture tool: catching one gives a Morning Dew for your pet. Killing one drops an EXP Stone instead.

Rewards

  • HardPoints for every run, in tiers by team score. Spend them at the Point Exchanger in the capitals.
  • Completing the run pays the top tier, plus a title: Seabed Explorer for a party, Deep Sea Hunter if you did it alone.
