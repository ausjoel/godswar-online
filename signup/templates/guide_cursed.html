The story

The people who lived here grew rich and stopped honouring the gods. The gods answered with a curse: the land's creatures grew stronger every year until the villagers had to leave. Their camps are still there, and so are the monsters.

Getting there

Talk to the Event Transporter in Athens or Sparta (use Search under the mini-map) and choose the Cursed Zone. There is no time limit and no daily entry: come and go as you like.

What to do there

  • Hunt. The camps' elites carry their own loot, including gear, so the Cursed Zone is a steady place to farm between quests.
  • Find Pan's Envoy in the camp. He is part of the Pan's Labyrinth event that opens at weekends (see the event calendar).
  • Bring friends. Party members nearby share experience and kill credit.

The old official game limited the Cursed Zone to levels 55-130. On this server anyone from level 20 can enter.
