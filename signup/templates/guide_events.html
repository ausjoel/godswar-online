Battlefields in brief

Battlefields pit Athens against Sparta. Kills and objectives score for your side, and when the round ends the Awarder pays Reputation and rewards, and titles that last a week. Ni Mini Valley is for levels 50-89, Pindus for 60 and up, and Lelantine Farm is a Saturday-night round with its own points, rewards and titles.

In the Trojan Expedition every kill on the Troy beach and in the city scores for you and your guild: 1 point for a normal Trojan, 5 for an elite and 10 for a hero. When the hour ends, claim your Exp Pills and your guild's Silver and Gold from an Event Awarder before 23:55. The top guild's members wear "Top Greek Guild" for a week.

Seasonal events such as Easter, May Day and Halloween switch on by themselves in the days before their dates and bring their own envoys and drops.
